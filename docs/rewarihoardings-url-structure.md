## PART 1 — OUTDOOR ADVERTISING FORMAT TAXONOMY (India Research)

### What "Outdoor Advertising" covers in India

Indian OOH is officially classified by OAAA-India and industry bodies into 4 macro categories:
1. **Large Format** — billboards, unipoles, gantries, wall painting
2. **Street Furniture** — bus shelters, pole kiosks, traffic booths, benches
3. **Transit Media** — vehicles, stations, roads
4. **Ambient / Non-Traditional** — society gates, construction hoardings, petrol pumps, kiosks

**Market size (2024):** ₹4,200 crore (traditional OOH) + ₹2,200 crore (DOOH) = ~₹6,400 crore total.
**Growth:** DOOH growing at 11.86% CAGR through 2033. Tier 2/3 cities now driving 40%+ of new OOH inventory.

## PART 4 — COMPLETE URL STRUCTURE

### URL Design Principles
1. Primary keyword first in URL — "rewari" appears where needed for SEO
2. Max 3 levels deep (no `/a/b/c/d/` nesting)
3. Every URL must be a standalone SEO-targetable page (not just navigation)
4. Locality × Type matrix = the core SEO engine
5. All lowercase, hyphen-separated, no trailing slashes
